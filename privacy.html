Tab CloakroomPrivacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Tab Cloakroom is a Chrome extension that puts unused tabs to sleep. Everything it keeps stays on your computer. It has no servers, no analytics and no account.

What it uses

DataWhyWhere it stays
Addresses and titles of your open tabsTo decide which tabs can sleep, and to keep a list of your tabs so you can reopen themOn your computer, inside the extension
Whether you typed on a page (yes or no)So a page you are typing in is not put to sleepIn that page while it is open, plus a yes/no note inside the extension until you leave the page
Your settingsTo remember your choicesOn your computer, inside the extension

Nothing is sent anywhere. The extension never reads what you type and never reads the content of pages.

How long it keeps things

The list of your tabs is kept for the period you choose (7, 30 or 90 days), then deleted. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

Read and change data on websites – used only to notice that you typed on a page. Nothing on the page is read or changed.

Tabs – used to see your open tabs so it can put them to sleep and keep the list of them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the previous version stays available in this site's history on GitHub.
