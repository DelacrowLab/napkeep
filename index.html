Napkeep

A free Chrome extension. Add it from the Chrome Web Store.

Puts tabs you are not using to sleep, so Chrome uses less memory. Click a sleeping tab and the page loads again.

What it will not do

  • Lose your tabs. It uses Chrome's own tab discarding and never rewrites a tab's address, so your tabs stay even if you remove it.
  • Put a tab to sleep while it notices you typing in it.
  • Send your tabs or history to us or to analytics services. There is no account.

Napkeep Pro

The free version keeps every promise above. Pro adds:

  • Workspaces: save a window's tabs under a name, close them, and open them again later in the same order. Tabs where you are typing are never closed.
  • Longer tab history: 90 days or a year, with search across saved titles and addresses.
  • Time per site: put some sites to sleep sooner or later than others.
