Refunds

Napkeep Pro

If Napkeep Pro is not right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying it. You do not need to give a reason, and you get the full amount back.

How

Reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or find the order at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders and contact us from there.

What happens after a refund

The license key stops working, and Pro features in the extension turn off. Workspaces and tab history you saved stay on your computer: you can still open and export them.

After 14 days we may still refund if something does not work as described; write to us and we will look at it.
